# First Installation Checklist

1. Charge battery and confirm firmware/health.
2. Confirm IMU and HX711 detection.
3. Connect the bridge and verify live strain response.
4. Perform Bench Calibration and verify direction.
5. Mount the enclosure and secure wiring.
6. Confirm USB-removal sleep, IMU wake, and USB wake.
7. Select Maintenance Mode, unplug USB, and rotate the crank by hand while watching live IMU data.
8. Tune cadence/revolution detection only from physical evidence.
9. Pair BLE and perform a short first ride.
10. Review diagnostics and disable temporary tuning/diagnostics.

The in-device Diagnostics page carries the same concise checklist.
